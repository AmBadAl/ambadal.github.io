  1. AAMAS
    Metric Distortion Under Public-Spirited Voting
    Amirreza Bagheridelouee, Marzie Nilipour, Masoud Seddighin, and Maziar Shamsipour
    In Proceedings of the 23rd International Conference on Autonomous Agents and Multiagent Systems, 2024
    Abs Bib PDF

    We investigate how public-spirited voting affects distortion in the metric framework. Using the public-spirited model introduced by Flanigan et al., we evaluate Plurality, Borda, Copeland, Veto, k-approval, and PluralityVeto. We establish a general lower bound for any voting rule and derive rule-specific lower and upper bounds. When all voters exhibit the same degree of public-spiritedness, PluralityVeto matches the general lower bound.
